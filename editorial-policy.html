Editorial Policy

Last reviewed: July 29, 2026

Free Typing Test Online is committed to publishing educational content that is accurate, practical, easy to understand, and regularly reviewed. Our goal is to help readers improve typing speed, typing accuracy, touch typing skills, and keyboard confidence through free resources.

Our mission: Make high-quality typing education freely available to students, professionals, job seekers, teachers, and anyone wanting to improve keyboard skills.

Editorial Principles

  • Accuracy before speed.
  • Clear explanations written for beginners.
  • Practical advice that readers can apply immediately.
  • Regular reviews to keep content current.
  • Transparent corrections when necessary.

How Content Is Created

Every guide begins with research into established typing techniques and educational best practices. Articles are then written, edited, reviewed, and checked for clarity before publication. Examples and practice exercises are designed to help readers apply concepts rather than simply explain them.

Research and Accuracy

We make reasonable efforts to ensure factual accuracy. When better information becomes available or mistakes are identified, pages are updated to improve accuracy, clarity, or usefulness.

Review and Updates

Content is reviewed periodically for relevance, broken links, readability, and search quality. Significant updates receive a revised review date.

Corrections Policy

If readers identify an error, we investigate it and make corrections when appropriate. Minor editorial improvements may be made without changing the meaning of the article.

Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions are based on educational value and user benefit. Articles are not written simply to promote products or services.

AI and Human Review

Technology may assist with drafting or editing, but published content is reviewed before being made available to readers. Final editorial responsibility remains with the site.

Author Information

Content is written and maintained by Alan for Free Typing Test Online. The focus of the site is practical typing education, typing tests, touch typing, keyboard skills, and typing improvement.

Reader Feedback

If you notice an error or have suggestions, please use our Contact page. Reader feedback helps improve the quality of our educational content.

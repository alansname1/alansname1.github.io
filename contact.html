Get in Touch

Thank you for visiting Free Typing Test Online. Messages about website problems, content corrections, accessibility, typing-test questions, and suggestions for new educational resources are welcome.

alan_plank@yahoo.com

Please use a clear subject line and include the address of the page involved when reporting a problem.

Before emailing

Do not send passwords, financial details, government identification numbers, medical information, or other sensitive personal data.

What You Can Contact Us About

Website Problems

Report broken links, missing pages, display problems, typing-test errors, or features that do not work as expected.

Content Corrections

Point out unclear wording, possible factual errors, outdated information, or content that could be more useful.

Accessibility

Report keyboard-navigation difficulties, contrast problems, screen-reader issues, or other accessibility barriers.

Resource Suggestions

Suggest typing exercises, practice topics, beginner guides, workplace resources, or other educational improvements.

Typing-Test Questions

Ask general questions about words per minute, accuracy, test duration, practice results, or how the site's tools work.

General Feedback

Share feedback about navigation, page design, readability, usefulness, or the overall visitor experience.

Details That Help Us Understand a Report

When reporting a website issue, include as much of the following information as you reasonably can:

  • the page URL or page title;
  • a brief description of what happened;
  • what you expected to happen;
  • the browser and device you were using; and
  • the wording of any error message you saw.

Screenshots may be helpful, but please remove or hide personal information before sending them.

Response Time

Messages are reviewed as time allows. A response is not guaranteed, but clear reports about website errors, accessibility barriers, and content corrections are prioritized when possible.

Free Typing Test Online does not provide emergency support, professional typing certification, employment placement, personalized medical advice, or legal advice.

Privacy reminder: Email is not always a secure communication method. Send only the information needed to explain your question or report.

Corrections and Editorial Feedback

Feedback that may improve the accuracy, clarity, or usefulness of an article is reviewed in line with the site's Editorial Policy. When reporting a possible error, please identify the page and explain the specific statement or section you believe should be reviewed.

How Contact Information May Be Used

Information you voluntarily include in an email may be used to read and respond to your message, investigate a reported issue, consider a suggestion, maintain communication records, or protect the website from abuse.

For more information, read the Privacy Policy.

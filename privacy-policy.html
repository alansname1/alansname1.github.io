Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, or shared when you visit Free Typing Test Online. It also explains your choices regarding cookies, advertising technologies, analytics services, and information that you choose to submit through direct communication.

Free Typing Test Online is designed primarily as an educational website. Visitors can use typing tests, read typing guides, and access practice resources without creating an account.

Important: The site does not currently require user registration or ask visitors to create personal profiles in order to use its typing tests and educational pages.

Information That May Be Collected

The website may collect limited information automatically when you visit. This information is generally technical or statistical rather than information that directly identifies you.

Type of information Examples Possible purpose
Device and browser information Browser type, operating system, screen size, and device category Improve compatibility, design, and performance
Usage information Pages viewed, time spent on pages, referring pages, and general navigation activity Understand which content is useful and improve the website
Approximate location Country, region, or city-level information inferred from an IP address Analytics, security, and regional service delivery
Technical logs IP address, access time, error logs, and request information Security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it, such as when you send an email or contact message.

Typing Test Results and Browser Storage

Some features may calculate or display information such as words per minute, typing accuracy, test duration, or progress results. Depending on the feature, this information may be processed directly in your browser.

If the website uses local storage or similar browser technology to remember results or preferences, that information may remain on your device until you clear your browser data. Local browser storage is not the same as creating a user account, and it may not be transmitted to the site owner unless a feature specifically requires that transmission.

Information You Provide Voluntarily

If you contact the site by email or through another communication method, your message may include your name, email address, and any other information you choose to provide.

This information may be used to respond to your question, investigate a reported issue, consider feedback, or maintain records of the communication. Please avoid sending sensitive personal, financial, medical, or account information.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files or identifiers that may be stored on your device by your web browser. The website or third-party services may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • remembering preferences;
  • measuring website traffic and performance;
  • understanding how visitors use pages;
  • supporting security and fraud prevention;
  • serving and measuring advertising; and
  • improving content and user experience.

Most browsers allow you to block, delete, or manage cookies through browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how certain website features work.

Analytics Services

Free Typing Test Online may use third-party analytics services to understand website traffic and general usage patterns. These providers may collect technical information using cookies or similar technologies.

Analytics information may be used to identify popular pages, detect errors, improve navigation, measure site performance, and better understand how visitors interact with educational content.

Advertising and Google Services

The website may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including Google. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, limit, or measure ads.

Google and other advertising vendors may use information about visits to this and other websites to provide advertising that may be more relevant to a visitor's interests. Depending on your location and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can manage certain advertising preferences through your browser, device settings, or the advertising controls made available by the relevant provider.

Why Information May Be Used

Depending on the type of information and applicable law, information may be processed for one or more of the following purposes:

  • to operate and maintain the website;
  • to improve content, performance, and usability;
  • to respond to messages or feedback;
  • to protect the website from fraud, abuse, or security threats;
  • to measure traffic and advertising performance;
  • to comply with legal obligations; or
  • with your consent when consent is required.

Third-Party Services

The website may rely on third-party providers for hosting, analytics, advertising, security, performance, or related technical services. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Free Typing Test Online does not control every data practice of independent third-party services. Visitors should review the privacy information provided by those services when appropriate.

External Links

Pages may contain links to other websites for reference or educational purposes. Once you leave Free Typing Test Online, the privacy practices of the destination website apply.

We are not responsible for the content, security, availability, or privacy practices of third-party websites.

How Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate, secure, analyze, or support the website. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or defend legal claims.

Free Typing Test Online does not sell personal information directly to data brokers. Advertising and analytics technologies may still involve data processing by third parties as described in this policy.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including website operation, communication, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and recordkeeping.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service provider involved.

Visitors are responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices, browsers, internet connections, and email accounts.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your browser, device, location, and applicable law, you may be able to:

  • block or delete cookies;
  • disable certain browser storage features;
  • limit ad personalization;
  • use private browsing or tracking-protection tools;
  • withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis for processing; or

Some choices may reduce website functionality or affect the relevance of advertisements.

Regional Privacy Rights

Privacy rights differ by country, state, and region. Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability, consent withdrawal, or complaints to a privacy authority.

Because Free Typing Test Online generally collects limited information directly, some requests may relate primarily to information processed by a third-party service provider. Requests will be considered in accordance with applicable law and the information reasonably available.

Children's Privacy

Free Typing Test Online provides general educational typing content but is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Children should not submit personal information through email or other contact methods without the involvement of a parent, guardian, teacher, or other responsible adult. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please use the Contact page.

Visitors may access the website from different countries. Information processed by third-party providers may be stored or handled in countries other than the visitor's own country, subject to the provider's policies and applicable safeguards.

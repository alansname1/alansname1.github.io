Typing Practice Tips

Simple techniques that can help you build faster, more accurate, and more consistent typing habits.

Written and reviewed by Alan • Last reviewed July 30, 2026

Top Typing Tips

  • Practice regularly.
  • Focus on accuracy before speed.
  • Use proper home-row finger placement.
  • Keep your eyes on the screen.
  • Review recurring mistakes after each session.
  • Take short breaks to avoid fatigue.
  • Use a mix of drills, paragraphs, and typing tests.
  • Track your weekly progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I improve typing faster?

Build a consistent routine, prioritize accuracy, and practice your weakest skills.

Should I practice every day?

Frequent practice is helpful, but choose a schedule you can maintain comfortably.

Is accuracy more important than speed?

Yes. Better accuracy usually supports sustainable improvements in typing speed.

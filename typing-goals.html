Typing Goals: Set Realistic Targets and Measure Progress

Create achievable typing goals that help you improve steadily.

Written and reviewed by Alan • Last reviewed July 30, 2026

Why Typing Goals Matter

Clear goals help you stay focused and measure improvement over time. Good goals balance speed, accuracy, consistency, and regular practice.

Examples of Typing Goals

GoalExample
SpeedIncrease from 35 to 45 WPM in 8 weeks.
AccuracyMaintain at least 98% accuracy.
ConsistencyPractice 20 minutes, 5 days each week.
EnduranceComplete a 10-minute typing test comfortably.

SMART Typing Goals

  • Specific: Improve one skill at a time.
  • Measurable: Track WPM and accuracy.
  • Achievable: Set realistic milestones.
  • Relevant: Match your school or work needs.
  • Time-bound: Review progress weekly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good beginner typing goal?

Build accuracy first, then increase speed gradually.

How often should I review my goals?

Review them weekly and update them as you improve.

Should I track accuracy?

Yes. Accuracy provides important context for your typing speed.

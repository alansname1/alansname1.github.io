What Is a Typing Speed Chart?

A typing speed chart is a simple reference that groups words-per-minute scores into broad skill ranges.

It can help you understand whether your current typing speed is still developing, practical for everyday tasks, or relatively fast.

However, WPM should not be treated as a strict ranking system. Typing performance also depends on:

  • Accuracy
  • Test length
  • Text difficulty
  • Punctuation
  • Numbers and symbols
  • Consistency
  • Keyboard familiarity
  • The type of task you are doing
Important: use the chart as a general guide, not a pass-or-fail standard.

General Typing Speed Chart

Typing Speed General Description What It May Mean
Under 20 WPM Early learner You may still be learning key locations or becoming comfortable with the keyboard.
20–30 WPM Beginner You can complete basic typing tasks but may still pause often or look at the keyboard.
30–40 WPM Developing Your typing is becoming more practical for everyday computer use.
40–50 WPM Practical A useful range for many school, email, form, and general office tasks.
50–60 WPM Good Comfortable typing speed for many regular computer users when accuracy is strong.
60–80 WPM Strong Efficient typing for many writing, office, school, and communication tasks.
80–100 WPM Very fast Fast typing that can be highly efficient when accuracy and consistency remain strong.
100+ WPM Exceptional speed A very high typing speed, especially when maintained accurately over longer tests.

These ranges are broad reference categories rather than formal standards.

Why the Same WPM Can Mean Different Things

Two people can both score 60 WPM and still have very different typing performance.

For example:

Typist A

60 WPM on a short test with many errors and frequent corrections.

Typist B

60 WPM with strong accuracy and similar results during longer tests.

Typist B may have more useful real-world typing performance even though both raw WPM scores are the same.

Typing Speed and Accuracy Together

WPM tells you how fast you typed. Accuracy tells you how reliably you typed.

Example Result Interpretation
45 WPM with strong accuracy Useful controlled typing with room to increase speed.
60 WPM with strong accuracy Good practical typing performance.
75 WPM with frequent mistakes Raw speed is high, but correction time may reduce practical performance.
80 WPM with strong accuracy Fast and efficient typing when the speed is repeatable.

What Does 20 WPM Mean?

Around 20 WPM often represents an early stage of keyboard development.

You may still:

  • Look at the keyboard frequently
  • Use only a few fingers
  • Pause while searching for letters
  • Make repeated mistakes

At this stage, technique and familiarity are more important than chasing higher speed.

The Typing for Beginners Guide can help you build the fundamentals.

What Does 30 WPM Mean?

Around 30 WPM can be a sign that basic keyboard familiarity is developing.

You may be able to complete everyday typing tasks, although longer writing may still feel slow.

A good next goal is often to reduce hesitation and improve accuracy rather than forcing a large speed increase immediately.

What Does 40 WPM Mean?

Around 40 WPM can be practical for many everyday computer tasks.

At this speed, users may be able to handle:

  • Email
  • School assignments
  • Online forms
  • Basic office work
  • Chat messages
  • General writing

Accuracy and consistency become increasingly important as your speed improves.

What Does 50 WPM Mean?

Around 50 WPM is a useful typing speed for many regular computer users.

At this stage, you may benefit more from improving:

  • Accuracy
  • Longer-session consistency
  • Punctuation
  • Numbers
  • More difficult vocabulary

A 5 Minute Typing Test can provide a better view of consistency than a very short test.

What Does 60 WPM Mean?

Around 60 WPM is a strong practical typing speed for many users.

Someone who can maintain approximately this speed accurately may be comfortable with substantial amounts of school, office, writing, communication, or remote work.

The next challenge is often maintaining the same quality during longer or more difficult typing.

What Does 80 WPM Mean?

Around 80 WPM represents fast typing for many people.

At this level, small improvements may depend less on simply moving faster and more on:

  • Reducing hesitation
  • Improving difficult word patterns
  • Maintaining accuracy
  • Developing rhythm
  • Handling punctuation smoothly

What Does 100+ WPM Mean?

Typing above 100 WPM is very fast.

However, reaching a high number during one short test is different from maintaining it accurately for several minutes.

When evaluating a very high score, consider:

  • Was accuracy strong?
  • Was the passage easy or difficult?
  • How long was the test?
  • Can you repeat the result?
  • Does your speed stay high with punctuation?

Typing Speed by Test Length

Test length can influence your result.

Test Best Use
1 minute Quick WPM check and short-term speed.
5 minutes Consistency and sustained accuracy.
10 minutes Endurance, concentration, and realistic long-form typing.

A personal best from a one-minute test may be higher than your comfortable long-form typing speed.

Typing Speed Goals by Current Level

Current Range Useful Goal

30–40 WPM Develop smoother sentence rhythm.

50–60 WPM Practice longer passages while maintaining accuracy.
60–80 WPM Improve difficult words, punctuation, and endurance.

How to Improve Your Position on the Chart

Do not try to jump from one category to another in a single session.

Instead:

  1. Take a baseline typing test.
  2. Record both WPM and accuracy.
  3. Identify repeated mistakes.
  4. Practice difficult keys and words.
  5. Use paragraphs to build realistic rhythm.
  6. Retest after several practice sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does WPM mean?

WPM means words per minute. It is commonly used to describe how quickly someone types.

What is a good typing speed?

It depends on the task, but many everyday computer users may find 40 to 60 WPM practical when accuracy is strong.

Is 60 WPM fast?

Around 60 WPM is a strong practical speed for many school, office, and everyday computer tasks when accuracy is also high.

Is 100 WPM professional?

There is no universal rule that defines a professional typist only by WPM. A score above 100 WPM is very fast, but professional usefulness also depends on accuracy, consistency, task requirements, and the ability to maintain performance.

Does accuracy affect my typing speed?

Yes. Mistakes require corrections and may reduce your real productivity even when your raw WPM is high.

Should I compare my WPM with other people?

A chart can provide context, but comparing your current performance with your own earlier results is often more useful for tracking improvement.

How to Stop Looking at the Keyboard While Typing

Train your eyes to stay on the screen and build reliable touch-typing habits.

Written and reviewed by Alan • Last reviewed July 30, 2026

Why We Look at the Keyboard

Most beginners rely on visual memory instead of finger memory. Touch typing replaces that habit through repetition and correct finger placement.

Helpful Tips

  • Keep your fingers on the home row.
  • Look at the screen instead of the keyboard.
  • Slow down to improve accuracy.
  • Practice for 15–30 minutes each day.
  • Use realistic paragraphs instead of random letters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to stop looking at the keyboard?

With consistent daily practice, many learners improve within a few weeks.

Should I cover my hands?

It can be useful if it helps you focus on the screen.

Will my typing speed decrease at first?

Yes. Speed often drops temporarily while building new muscle memory.
